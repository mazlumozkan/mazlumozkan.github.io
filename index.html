About

I am a PhD candidate in Sociology at the University of Groningen. My research focuses on protest and social movements: how movements respond to repression, and how state violence shapes collective action.

My work also examines political discourse, emotions and symbolic practices in protest, combining theory with computational text and visual analysis. My current research examines the emotional dynamics of protest imagery during Iran's Woman, Life, Freedom movement. My broader interests include Iranian and Middle Eastern politics and great-power politics in the region. I am affiliated with the SCOOP programme and the Interuniversity Center for Social Science Theory and Methodology (ICS).
