Research

My research focuses on protest and social movements. I study how movements respond to repression and how state violence shapes collective action. My work also examines political discourse, emotions and symbolic practices in protest, and I combine theory with computational text and visual analysis.

My current research examines the emotional dynamics of protest imagery during Iran's Woman, Life, Freedom movement. Alongside this, I study recent youth movements in Europe, using France as a case study. My broader interests include Iranian and Middle Eastern politics and great-power politics in the region.
